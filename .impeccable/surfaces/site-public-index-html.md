# perchbrowser.com landing page (beta)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: Mac users who live in the browser and already pay for ChatGPT, Claude or Grok. Action: download the v0.1.0 beta (GitHub pre-release DMG). Proof: real screenshots of the real app; no invented numbers, speed stays as stated 1.0 targets.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page opens as Perch's own new tab page: the visitor's question is already in the Search | Ask box, Perch answers in two sentences, and the answer ends in an approval card whose confirm button is the download. It refuses the category default of a headline over a floating device mockup and a feature-card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Perch's Ink theme (canvas #000, card #0E0E10, elevated #1A1A1E, ink #FCFDFF, mute #A1A4A5, white-6/14% hairlines), Blue accent #4C8DFF only on the perch dot, focus and links. Geist UI, Geist Serif for the one display headline, Geist Mono for versions and meta. Controls are Perch's: 12px-radius address box, segmented Search | Ask, approval card on elevated with hairline-strong, primary button ink on black. Paper theme for light-mode visitors.

STORY: The visitor learns what Perch is from Perch answering, believes it because every later section is a real screenshot of the real app (side chat with an approval card, permission modes, models, new tab, appearance), each captioned as sample data. Frosted menus, sleeping tabs and Spaces are described in copy, not shown: the snapshot tool copies the browser window's layer, and menus are separate native windows. Owner, 2026-11-09, asked whether to capture them first: "Ship without them (Recommended)", and downloads from the card at the top or the closing card.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim nav (mark, Features, Privacy, GitHub, small Download). Centred 720px column: 48px Perch mark, Geist Serif headline at ~56px, the 56px address box with the question typed and Ask selected, 12px hint line, a two-sentence answer in body, then the approval card: "Download Perch beta", mono meta line (v0.1.0 · 197 MB · macOS 13+ · Apple silicon · signed and notarized), primary "Download for Mac" plus "View source on GitHub". The card's top edge sits above the fold at 1280x800.

FORM: Ask the address bar, position 6 on my ordered list; seed:d40a1e5940bb2a01. Signature interaction: on load the question types into the box and the answer and card fade in (instant under Reduce Motion, content present without JS). Motion: Perch's curve cubic-bezier(0.2,0,0,1), 180–240ms.
