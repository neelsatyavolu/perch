# OpenTable

## Find the restaurant

Open `https://www.opentable.com`, search for the restaurant or cuisine and city, then set party size, date and time in the search bar or on the restaurant's page.

## Read availability

Check the party size, date and time in the search bar match the task before reading results: OpenTable keeps the last search. The time buttons under each restaurant are the open slots; read them all, since they are often just around the asked time.

## Booking

Clicking a time opens the booking form. Fill in what the task gives; the user's saved details may already be there. Completing the reservation waits for the user's approval. If a deposit or card is required, say so before going further.

## Notes

- "No online availability" means none on OpenTable, not that the restaurant is full; say that and give the phone number from its page.
- Experiences and ticketed events show separately from ordinary tables.
